The approach

We build your AI team from zero to one.

Deploy. Build. Hand off.

We succeed the day you can run it without us.

01

We set the foundation

Whatever shape your codebase is in, there's no judgment here. We've seen worse, and we know how to fix it, so whoever builds next inherits something solid instead of a mess.

02

We make sure the work has an owner

Sometimes that's a permanent team we help you hire, from our network of vetted experts or candidates you source. Sometimes it's your own engineers picking up a proof of concept and running with it. The handoff fits where you actually are.

03

We build it, then train your team to take over

You get working systems and the full context to run them. Our in-house agentic memory tracks every decision as we build, so nothing lives only in an engineer's head. We train your team on everything we've built, every transition is clean, and we stay on as backup for as long as you want us, and no longer.
